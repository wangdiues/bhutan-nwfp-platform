Platform overview

A connected knowledge, planning, market & traceability portal for Bhutan's NWFP sector.

This platform brings together management plans, Dzongkhag coverage, NWFP groups, product opportunities, spatial resources, buyer enquiries, and training content for sustainable non-wood forest product development.
