Featured NWFP

Cordyceps sinensis

The most economically significant NWFP in Bhutan, closely tied to high-altitude collection, rural cash income, and careful conservation controls.

Mushroom

Matsutake

Premium edible mushroom with strong export potential.

Medicinal herb

Paris polyphylla

Valuable medicinal plant requiring traceability and sustainable harvest planning.

Dye and medicinal

Rubia cordifolia

Natural dye and medicinal uses, with strong value-addition opportunities beyond raw collection.

Aromatic resource

Juniperus recurva

Himalayan aromatic associated with incense, ritual use, and local livelihoods.
