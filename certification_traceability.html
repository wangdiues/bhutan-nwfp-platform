Compliance chain

01

Step 1

Plan & Allocation

Confirm active PA, CFMP, or FMU-aligned NWFP management plan and permitted product. Verify Dzongkhag, DFO, or park office alignment.

02

Step 2

Harvest Record

Record group, collector, location, season, quota, batch reference, and supervising DFO or park office for complete chain of custody.

03

Step 3

Quality Control

Check drying, sorting, grading, packaging, contamination, storage, and shelf-life requirements to buyer specification.

04

Step 4

Market Release

Attach all documents, certification status, transport details, and buyer enquiry records for full compliance at point of sale.

Record keeping

Audit-ready documentation

All records are structured for internal audit and external buyer verification. Batch codes link to original harvest records and plans.

Quality

Grading standards

Products are graded against species-specific standards for moisture, sorting, size, and contamination before market release.

Connected records
