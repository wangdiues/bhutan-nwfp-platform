Compliance flow

From approved management plan to buyer-ready product.

Traceability connects harvest permits, group records, quotas, quality checks, packaging, and buyer documentation.

Step 1

Plan and allocation

Confirm active PA, CFMP, or FMU-aligned NWFP management plan and permitted product.

Step 2

Harvest record

Record group, collector, location, season, quota, batch, and supervising DFO or park office.

Step 3

Quality control

Check drying, sorting, grading, packaging, contamination, storage, and shelf-life requirements.

Step 4

Market release

Attach documents, product certification status, transport details, and buyer enquiry records.
